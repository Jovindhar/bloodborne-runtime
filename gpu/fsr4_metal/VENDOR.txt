Vendored from https://github.com/UzenUPoZiTiV4ik/fsr4-native-macos commit
329d1d7367b6b948a53486505198a5d254f02809 (MIT License, below), from its src/:
fsr411_helpers.metal, fsr411_simd.metal, fsr411_mpp.metal, fsr411_model.h, fsr411_runtime.h,
fsr411_scratch_layout.h, fsr4_device.h, fsr4_input.h, fsr4_log.h. Unchanged except
fsr411_runtime.h, whose local changes are marked "bbport:": it loads only what build_assets.py
built (no compiling or Python while the game runs), takes AMD's initializers from the player's
folders after checking their SHA-256, and assembles the native weights from the build's recipe
(fold_conv3x3 ported from its tools/fsr411/lossy.py, winograd from tools/gen_fsr411_native.py).
Its Lossy 2 profile is not used: its trained weights are a separate GPL download.

build_assets.py (ours) runs that commit's tools/compile_fsr411.py and tools/gen_fsr411_native.py
at build time, from the tree scripts/macos/build-deps.sh puts in deps/share/fsr4-native-macos
(without its prebuilt binaries or AMD's initializers).
